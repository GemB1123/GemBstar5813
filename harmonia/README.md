# Harmonia plates

The 29 plates of Andreas Cellarius, Harmonia Macrocosmica (1660), used by the Harmonia World of the Earthstrology Device and the Harmonia phone app.

Scans courtesy of the Biblioteca comunale di Trento, via Wikimedia Commons (Category: Cellarius Harmonia Macrocosmica). Public domain. `plates/` are 2600 px wide, `thumbs/` 480 px, numbered in the page order of the Trento copy.

`index.html` is the phone app test build.
